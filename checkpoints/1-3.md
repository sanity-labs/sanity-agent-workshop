# Checkpoint 1-3 · Add a knowledge base as a second source

_Correct if you're still on the Green & Gather seed and using the shared KB._

**The route.** Two MCP clients: the GROQ one with `SANITY_ORGANIZATION_TOKEN`, the KB one with
`SANITY_CONTEXT_KB_TOKEN`. `initial_context` stripped or renamed from at least one before merging,
so both tool sets survive. Tool list now includes `knowledge_base_read` alongside `groq_query`.

**"Is the Thai Crunch Bowl peanut-free?"** One `knowledge_base_read` call with the KB id and one
or two paths from the outline (something like a Thai Crunch allergen entry and the cross-contact
statement). The answer says the bowl contains no peanut **ingredients**, that its tamarind-lime
sauce is made on equipment that also processes peanuts and the supplier **cannot certify** it free
of peanut protein, cites the **Sunfield Foods spec sheet**, and ends with the cross-contact
statement. It does **not** say "peanut-free" or "safe".

**If the agent used `groq_query` instead** and answered "yes, no peanut declared" — that is the
confidently wrong answer the mission is built around, and it means the agent chose the wrong
source. Correct observation; routing is Mission 1-4.

**"Is your fryer shared?"** One KB lookup. Yes — shared fryer and prep surfaces — quoting the
prep standard or cross-contact statement.

**"Can I get something gluten-free?"** **One** `knowledge_base_read` where Mission 1-2 took
several `groq_query` calls. The answer names the `gluten-free-option` items as options, says the
current substitution policy does **not** promise a gluten-free wrap swap because of shared prep
surfaces, notes the March 2025 operations manual said otherwise and is superseded, and may cite
the Northfield tortilla sheet.

**If the agent says the swap is available on request**, it read the manual over the policy. That
is a KB `instructions` lever for the facilitator, not something to fix in your repo — tell a
helper.

**Errors:** a `403` naming the knowledge base means the wrong token went to the KB endpoint;
`-32005` means the URL is the GROQ endpoint.
