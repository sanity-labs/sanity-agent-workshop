_Correct if you're still on the Green & Gather seed. Run `pnpm seed:reset` first._

**The clean item — Herb Falafel Wrap.** Its recipe reveals wheat and sesame; the menu declares
wheat and sesame. The log shows, in order: a delta check that finds no undeclared allergens;
Generate; a patch of the base copy into the three market fields; Transform; a success notification
listing the fields that are actually populated. In the Studio draft:

- `description.base` populated.
- `description.nyc`, `description.austin`, `description.chicago` all populated and **visibly
  different** in voice — NYC tighter and more polished, Austin warmer and more casual, Chicago
  hearty and neighborhood-flavored. The dish facts are identical across all three.
- `allergenCallout` populated by Generate and **not** varied by market.

**The planted case — Harissa Chickpea Bowl.** The log shows the delta check finding **sesame**
undeclared, then a structured warning naming the item and `["sesame"]`, and **no** Generate or
Transform. In the Studio, no market copy exists for it, and whatever flag the handler used is
visible (a held-for-review note on the draft, or the item moved to `internal`). The Function did
not fire again after its own flag — one invocation in the log, not sixteen.

**If the three market fields are empty but Transform reported success**, the patch step is
missing — Transform is path-for-path and cannot read `base` into `nyc`. **If the three variants
are near-identical**, the per-target instructions didn't name the difference — a prompt fix, not
a code fix. **If `allergenCallout` has market variants**, the
Transform targeted it; it shouldn't. **If the Harissa bowl got copy**, the delta check isn't
running first. **If the Function fired repeatedly**, the flag wrote to the published document
inside the blueprint filter's scope without a guard.
