2. Product description

A shelf-stable, sweet-sour finishing sauce built on tamarind concentrate and lime, seasoned with naturally brewed soy sauce, garlic, ginger and a small quantity of red chili. Smooth, pourable, glossy, dark amber to brown in colour. Intended to be applied cold as a finishing sauce or tossed through cooked grains, vegetables and proteins immediately before service. Not intended for use as a marinade requiring heat processing by the customer.

3. Ingredient declaration

Water, tamarind concentrate, cane sugar, lime juice concentrate, naturally brewed soy sauce (water, soybeans, wheat, salt), rice vinegar, garlic, ginger, red chili, modified corn starch, salt, xanthan gum, citric acid, natural flavour.

Ingredients listed in descending order of predominance by weight, per 21 CFR 101.4. The declaration above is the approved statement for use on customer-facing menus and labels. Any abbreviation of this statement must be approved in writing by Sunfield Foods Regulatory Affairs.

9. Shelf life, storage and handling
