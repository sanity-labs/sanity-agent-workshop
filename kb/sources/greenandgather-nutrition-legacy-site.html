Nutrition & Allergen Information

Last updated: November 2024

Welcome to the Green & Gather nutrition site. Everything we serve starts with whole vegetables, real grains and sauces we're proud of, and we think you should be able to see exactly what goes into your bowl. This site collects the nutrition and allergen details for our full menu in one place, so you can plan your order before you reach the counter.

We're happy to accommodate most dietary needs and allergies — just ask a team member. Our crews are trained on every item on the menu and can help you build something that works for you, whether you're avoiding dairy, eating plant-based, or managing a food allergy.

Looking for something specific?
  • Allergen Matrix — every menu item against the nine major allergens, plus calories per serving.
  • Our Ingredient Promise — how we source and prepare what we serve.
  • Seasonal Menu — what's on now and what we've served in past seasons.

How to read our nutrition information

Calorie counts are for a standard build with the default grain, protein and sauce. Swapping or removing a component will change the numbers, and our team can help you estimate the difference. Allergen information reflects the ingredients in each item's recipe as of the date at the top of this page.

Plant-based and vegetarian

Every item marked vegan on our menu boards is made entirely without animal products, including honey. Items marked vegetarian may contain dairy, egg or honey. Several of our bowls and salads are naturally plant-based, and most others can be made so by leaving off the cheese or yogurt drizzle.

Gluten

Many of our bowls and salads are built without wheat-containing ingredients. Keep in mind that farro is a wheat grain, and that soy sauce and miso often contain wheat. If you're avoiding gluten, a bowl on brown rice or quinoa is your best starting point — and just ask a team member about your options at the wrap station.

Notes

Our Ingredient Promise

Last updated: November 2024

Green & Gather started with a simple idea: fast food should be real food. Five years on, that still means whole vegetables cut in our kitchens each morning, grains cooked fresh every day, and sauces made from recipes we developed ourselves rather than bought off a shelf.

Vegetables, cut daily

Our produce arrives washed from regional growers and is cut, roasted and charred in-store. Nothing comes out of a bag pre-chopped. Kale is stemmed and shredded by hand; broccoli and cauliflower are roasted hot enough to char the edges; sweet potatoes are cut into wedges and roasted with rosemary. If it's a vegetable, someone in the building touched it this morning.

Grains, cooked fresh

Brown rice, quinoa and farro are cooked in small batches throughout the day, never held overnight. Farro is a whole wheat grain with a chewy bite; if you're avoiding wheat, brown rice and quinoa are naturally wheat-free alternatives on any bowl.

Sauces we're proud of

Our tahini-based sauces and dressings, the yogurt drizzle, the caesar base and the miso-ginger dressing are produced in our own central kitchen from recipes developed by our culinary team, then delivered fresh to every location. A small number of specialty items, like our tamarind-lime sauce and chili crisp, come from partner producers we've worked with for years and whose ingredients we review line by line.

Proteins

Our chicken is antibiotic-free thigh meat, marinated and grilled. Our tofu is firm, marinated and roasted for texture. Both arrive from long-standing partners and are portioned to order in the kitchen.

Allergens and food safety

We know that for many guests, the ingredient list isn't a curiosity — it's a health question. Every Green & Gather kitchen follows strict allergen protocols to keep your meal safe. Our teams are trained on every item on the menu, allergy orders are flagged and prepared with fresh gloves and clean tools, and our allergen matrix is kept up to date as recipes change. If you have an allergy, tell us when you order and we'll take care of the rest.

What we don't do

No artificial colours. No high-fructose corn syrup. No peanuts or tree nuts in any recipe. No frying in partially hydrogenated oils. And no menu item we wouldn't serve to our own families.
