10-Inch Flour Tortilla, Foodservice

Pressed and baked wheat flour tortilla · Ambient · Customer: Green & Gather Restaurants LLC

Manufacturing site Northfield Bakehouse, Allentown, PA — Tortilla Hall, Lines T1 and T2
Certifications BRCGS Food Safety, Issue 9 (Grade AA). Kosher pareve (OU).

2. Product description

A soft, pliable, die-cut wheat flour tortilla, hot-pressed and baked on a three-tier gas oven, cooled and packed in sleeves of 12. Pale cream colour with light toast marks on both faces. Neutral flavour with a mild wheat note. Suitable for cold wrap assembly and for griddling or pressing at the customer's point of service.

3. Ingredient declaration

Enriched bleached wheat flour (wheat flour, niacin, reduced iron, thiamine mononitrate, riboflavin, folic acid), water, vegetable shortening (palm oil, soybean oil), contains 2% or less of: salt, sugar, leavening (sodium bicarbonate, sodium acid pyrophosphate, corn starch, monocalcium phosphate), dough conditioners (mono- and diglycerides, fumaric acid, sodium metabisulfite), calcium propionate and sorbic acid (preservatives), cellulose gum.

Highly refined soybean oil is exempt from allergen labelling under FALCPA §203(qq)(2) and is not declared as an allergen. Contains sulfites (sodium metabisulfite) below 10 ppm in the finished product.

9. Shelf life, storage and handling

Shelf life, ambient 45 days from date of manufacture at 18 – 24 °C (65 – 75 °F), sleeve unopened
Shelf life, refrigerated 90 days at ≤ 4 °C (40 °F), sleeve unopened
Shelf life, frozen 180 days at ≤ −18 °C (0 °F). Thaw sealed at ambient for 4 hours before use. Do not refreeze.
Opened sleeve Use within 3 days; reseal and hold at ambient or refrigerated
Point-of-service handling Bring to room temperature before rolling. Warm on griddle or press for 10 – 15 seconds per side for best pliability.
Date coding Best-by date (MMDDYY) and line code ink-jetted on sleeve seal and case end panel
